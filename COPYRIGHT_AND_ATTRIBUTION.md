## Origin of HIERSTEP

The HIERSTEP hierarchical simulation was originally developed by Howard T. Odum and published as a BASIC program in:

Odum, H. T. 2001. "Simulating Emergy and Materials in Hierarchical Steps." In *Emergy Synthesis 1: Proceedings of the First Biennial Emergy Conference*, edited by M. T. Brown, 119–127. Gainesville, Florida: Center for Environmental Policy.

The original model and BASIC program are attributed to H. T. Odum.

## Python Adaptation

This repository contains a Python adaptation of Odum's HIERSTEP program. The Python version was developed with assistance from Cursor AI, under the direction and review of Thomas Abel.

Thomas Abel's contributions include:
- Directing the Python adaptation and reviewing and editing the resulting code.
- Adding variables and calculations for further evaluation, as identified in the technical documentation.
- Directing the development of the graphical user interface.

The technical documentation and accompanying academic paper describe these additions and their relationship to Odum's original program.
